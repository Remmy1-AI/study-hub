Docket Privacy Policy

Docket by Mr. Yeboah · Android app id study.mryeboah.docket
Last updated: 26 November 2026

Docket is a study app for UMAT students. It lets you browse and read course materials shared by Mr. Yeboah, save them for offline use, and pick up where you left off. This policy explains what information Docket collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Phone number. You sign in with your mobile phone number. We send a one-time passcode (OTP) by SMS to verify it. Your number is your account identifier.
  • Profile details you enter. Your name and the programme you study, entered when you sign up.
  • Reading progress. Which materials you have opened, the page you reached, the total pages, and when you last read.
  • Bookmarks. The materials and pages you bookmark.
  • Downloads. Which materials you have downloaded for offline use, so your list follows your account. We also keep an aggregate download count per material.

Browsing the catalogue does not require an account. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

2. How we use it

  • To verify your identity with a phone OTP and keep you signed in.
  • To show your name in the app and personalise your home screen.
  • To save and restore your reading progress, bookmarks and downloads across sessions and devices.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and to understand which materials are useful.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run Docket and only share what is needed for them to do so:

  • Supabase hosts our authentication and database (your account, profile, progress, bookmarks and downloads). Data is protected so that each signed-in user can read and change only their own progress, bookmarks and downloads.
  • BulkClix delivers the OTP text message to your phone number.
  • Expo builds and delivers the app.

4. Data on your device

Docket stores your sign-in session in the device's secure storage, and keeps downloaded files and some preferences on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data (phone number, profile, progress, bookmarks, downloads) at any time using the contact below, and we will do so within a reasonable time. Standard SMS delivery records may be kept briefly by the carrier or SMS provider under their own policies.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Docket is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

You can use the catalogue without signing in, sign out at any time, remove downloads from within the app, and request access to or deletion of your data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
