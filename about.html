About Ace with Mr. Yeboah

Learn • Share • Succeed

Ace with Mr. Yeboah is a study-resources hub for students at the University of Mines and Technology (UMAT). Find course slides, lecture notes, past questions, tutorials, and peer materials — organised by programme.

It is built by Frank Yeboah, an Electrical student at UMAT, for every programme on campus — not just engineering.

How materials are organised

Programme→ Level→ Course→ Material

Example: Electrical → Level 200 → EE 201 → Past Questions.

FAQs

Who is this for?

All UMAT students across the programmes listed on this site.

Is this an official university site?

No — it is a student-led knowledge bank run by Frank Yeboah.

How do I search?

Use the search box on the home page, or filters on Programmes and Resources.

How do materials get added?

Frank publishes through Admin. A student contribution form comes in a later phase.

Contact
